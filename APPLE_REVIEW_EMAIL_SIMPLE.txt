COMPLIANCE CHECKLIST

1. Age Rating: 18+ ✅
We have updated our app's age rating to 18+ in App Store Connect as required for anonymous user-generated content applications.

2. Terms Agreement with Zero-Tolerance Policy ✅
Location: Displayed immediately on first app launch (before any content is accessible)
Implementation:
- Users MUST accept terms to proceed
- Clear statement: "Peekio has a zero-tolerance policy for objectionable content or abusive users"
- Checkbox required: "I agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy"
- Links to full Terms of Service and Privacy Policy provided

How to Test: Launch app for the first time → Terms screen appears before any other content

3. Content Filtering Method ✅
Implementation: Automated reputation system with escalating actions
- User receives 3 reports → Status changes to "flagged"
- User receives 5 reports → Status changes to "restricted"
- Restricted users → Cannot send new content, existing content automatically removed
- All content from flagged/restricted users is filtered from feeds

How to Test: Backend system (Firebase Firestore) with automatic enforcement

4. User Flagging Mechanism ✅
Location: Available on all user-generated content screens
Implementation:
- When viewing received content → Tap "Report" button (visible on image view screen)
- Report options include: inappropriate content, abusive behavior, etc.
- All reports stored in backend with timestamp and reason
- Reports automatically update user reputation

How to Test:
1. Receive a peek from another user
2. View the image
3. Look for "Report" or flag icon on screen

5. User Blocking Mechanism ✅
Location: Available on all user-generated content screens
Implementation:
- Users can block any sender
- Blocked users cannot send future content to the blocker
- Block list maintained in user profile
- One-tap block functionality

How to Test:
1. Receive content from a user
2. Look for "Block" option on the content screen
3. Blocked users will not appear in future interactions

6. Immediate Content Removal ✅
Implementation: Automatic and manual removal options
- Users can immediately dismiss any received content
- Content from flagged/restricted users automatically removed from all feeds
- Reports trigger immediate evaluation and potential removal
- No content persists after user dismisses it

How to Test: Content disappears immediately upon dismissal or block action

7. 24-Hour Response Commitment ✅
Location: Settings → Contact & Support page
Implementation:
- Explicit statement displayed: "We respond to all reports within 24 hours as required by App Store guidelines"
- Dedicated email addresses for different types of reports:
  - safety@peekio.app (urgent safety issues)
  - support@peekio.app (general support)
  - technical-support@peekio.app (technical issues)
- Admin dashboard for report review and action within 24 hours

How to Test:
1. Open app
2. Navigate to Settings (bottom navigation)
3. Tap "Contact & Support"
4. See 24-hour commitment statement and all contact methods

8. Developer Contact Information ✅
Location: Settings → Contact & Support (prominently displayed in app)
Implementation:
- Full "Contact & Support" page with multiple reporting options
- "Report Inappropriate Content" - direct access to safety team
- "Report User Behavior" - for abusive users
- "Emergency Safety Issues" - for urgent concerns
- Email addresses clearly displayed:
  - support@peekio.app
  - safety@peekio.app
  - technical-support@peekio.app

How to Test:
1. Open app
2. Tap "Settings" (bottom right navigation)
3. Scroll to "Legal & Support" section
4. Tap "Contact & Support"
5. See comprehensive reporting and contact options
